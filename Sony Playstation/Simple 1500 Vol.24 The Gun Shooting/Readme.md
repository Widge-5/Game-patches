**An English translation patch for Simple 1500 Series Vol. 24: The Gun Shooting, originally released for the Japanese PlayStation.**

**The patch translates the game’s Japanese text, including menu items, and stage briefings. The audio speech in the small animated intro has not been replaced, but English subtitles have been added.**
**The patch also adds a new option to the game: to swap the functions of the GunCon's A & B buttons, to suit a typical western layout.**


The game features only a few lines of spoken dialogue, and they are all in the brief intro animation.  These have not been redubbed into English (replacing the video would increase the size of the patch), but I have subtitled them.  Interestingly, the game's files includes many lines of character speech that appear to have been originally intended to be heard during gameplay, particularly boss encounters, but were left unused in the final released game.
Perhaps in the future, if people wish to lend their voices to an English audio translation, I may update the patch to incorporate the speech.


The completed patch has passed a full playthrough with no problems reported.


**Patching**

This patch was created using a CUE/BIN image, consisting of a single BIN file, ripped from my own copy of the Japanese game (SLPS-01106) using ImgBurn.

Use PPF-O-Matic to apply `The Gun Shooting (SLPS-02474) English translation patch v1.0.ppf` to the unmodified BIN file. Do not apply it to the CUE file or an already-patched image.

The release includes the translation patch and the fully translated, proofread English manual.
