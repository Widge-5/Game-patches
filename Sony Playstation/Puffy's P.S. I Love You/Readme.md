An English translation patch for **Puffy’s P.S. I Love You**, originally released for the Japanese PlayStation.

The patch translates the game’s Japanese text, including menus, minigame instructions, lightgun calibration prompts, memory-card messages, results screens, song titles and Japanese lettering in the graphics.

Graphical text has been redrawn to follow the original artwork’s style and colours, with adjustments to sizing and positioning to accommodate the English wording.

The original English song titles have been used where available.
The songs themselves have not been translated.
The game includes two short video clips with spoken Japanese. Congratulatory messages from the girls that are selected at random and played after succesfully completing the can shooting game. English subtitles are provided for these.

Original music videos, photographs, music and audio are preserved. The game’s rules, scoring, difficulty and controls have not been changed.


**Patching**

This patch was created using a CUE/BIN image, consisting of a single BIN file, ripped from the of the original Japanese game disc (**ESPM-70003**) using imgBurn.

Use PPF-O-Matic to apply the PPF3 patch to the unmodified BIN file. Do not apply it to the CUE file or an already-patched image.


The release includes the translation patch and the fully translated, proofread English manual.
